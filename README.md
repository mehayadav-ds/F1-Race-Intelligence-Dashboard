# F1-Race-Intelligence-Dashboard
Tableau dashboard and story for Formula 1 race intelligence analysis.

## Problem statement
This project analyses Formula 1 race data across 17 seasons to support three scenarios:
1. Team performance strategy (points trajectory, tyre compounds by circuit type, talent by nationality)
2. Broadcast analysis (driver leaderboard, GP by country, podium distribution)
3. FIA reliability analysis (driver DNF reasons, DNF by team, reliability over seasons)

## Dataset
F1 Racing Project Dataset (Excel), with five tables: circuits, championship_standings, lap_intelligence, qualifying_results and race_results. The analysis uses race_results.

## Tools
Tableau Desktop

## Preprocessing
- Podium: finish position of 3 or better
- Nationality: driver grouped into country
- Reliability %: share of drivers who finished
- Season and DNF converted to dimensions

## Contents
- F1 Race Intelligence.twbx: Tableau workbook with 9 sheets, a dashboard and a story
- F1 Race Intelligence Dashboard.png: picture of the dashboard
- Video: link to the explanation video

## Key insights
- Mercedes and Red Bull lead the team points trajectory.
- Max Verstappen has the most points in the driver leaderboard.
- Reliability and DNFs vary by team and season.
